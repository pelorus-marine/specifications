## Our Pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers of Pelorus, pledge to make participation in this project a respectful, constructive, and sailor-first experience for everyone.

Pelorus exists to create a reliable, open marine data network. We value technical excellence, honesty, long-term repairability, and the real-world needs of offshore sailors above all else.

---

## Our Standards

**Expected behavior:**

- Be clear, direct, and technically accurate
- Focus on the merits of the work, not the person
- Give and receive constructive feedback
- Respect the locked architectural decisions in `ARCHITECTURE.md` and documents 02–10
- Prioritize reliability, simplicity, and debuggability for v1.0
- Acknowledge uncertainty rather than guess

**Unacceptable behavior:**

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind
- Trolling, flaming, or inflammatory language
- Deliberately pushing changes that relitigate locked decisions without maintainer invitation
- Publishing misleading or false claims about conformance
- Disrespecting the sailor-first philosophy (e.g. prioritizing vendor lock-in over openness)

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.

Instances of unacceptable behavior will result in:
1. A private warning
2. Temporary ban from the repository (if repeated)
3. Permanent ban from all Pelorus repositories and communication channels (in extreme cases)

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all Pelorus repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and public communication channels operated by the pelorus-marine organization.

---

## Reporting

To report unacceptable behavior, open a private issue in any Pelorus repository titled “Code of Conduct report” or contact the project maintainer directly via GitHub.

All reports will be reviewed promptly and confidentially.
