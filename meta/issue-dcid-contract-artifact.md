# DCID contract artifact (tracking)

**Status:** placeholder — no committed generator yet.

Cross-links to [`CONTRIBUTING.md` § DCID numbering](../CONTRIBUTING.md#dcid-numbering-registry-and-vss-linkage).

When Pelorus defines or publishes a machine-readable **DCID contract** artifact (for example a schema, registry snapshot, or generated bundle consumed by compilers and gateways), changes that alter DCID assignment or semantics must land in the **same pull request** as:

- updates to [`core/07-dcid-registry.md`](../core/07-dcid-registry.md) and related catalog entries, and  
- regeneration or versioning of that artifact in-repo.

Exploratory structure and naming remain discussed in [Issue #3](https://github.com/pelorus-marine/specifications/issues/3) until promoted into normative documents.
